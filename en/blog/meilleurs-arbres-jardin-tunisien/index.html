The best trees and plants for a Tunisian garden

Published on 18 July 2026

Between summer heat and mild winters, certain species stand out for composing a resilient, attractive Tunisian garden.

Our picks for a resilient garden

  • The decorative palm tree, for an immediate structuring effect
  • The weeping fig and rubber fig, for dense greenery
  • The Madagascar dragon tree, graphic and low water need
  • The travelers tree, for a distinct exotic touch
  • A natural lawn carpet to unify the whole garden

Composing a coherent garden

Combining one or more palm trees, decorative shrubs and a well-maintained natural lawn structures a Tunisian garden without multiplying fragile or high-maintenance species.

Fast-growing trees in Tunisia: for shade and privacy in just a few years

Published on 29 July 2026

When you don't want to wait decades to enjoy some shade or privacy, certain fast-growing species deliver a visible result in just a few years.

Why choose a fast-growing tree?

  • Get shade on a terrace or relaxation area without waiting decades
  • Quickly create a green screen to preserve your privacy
  • Fill out a new garden that still lacks plant volume
  • Support a landscaping project that needs a visible effect in the short term

What to check before planting

Fast growth often comes with a vigorous root system: it is important to allow enough distance from foundations, pipework and shared walls. A fast-growing tree also generally needs heavier watering in the first year, while its roots settle in deeply.

Frequently asked questions

Does a fast-growing tree need more care?

Mostly in the first year: heavier watering supports good rooting. Once established, care is generally similar to other ornamental trees.

Should a fast-growing tree be staked?

This is often recommended for the first few months, while the root system develops enough to stabilise the tree on its own.

Frequently asked questions about delivery

Why is lawn delivery free in only two zones?

Greater Tunis and Nabeul are the zones closest to our depot, which allows us to offer free lawn delivery at no extra cost. For other regions, a transport rate is calculated based on distance.

Can the palm tree be delivered anywhere in Tunisia?

Yes, we deliver the decorative palm tree across the whole country. Delivery fees are determined based on your region and communicated before order confirmation.
